## Shipping (Settings → Shipping and delivery)
AliExpress items ship from China or EU warehouses, so delivery takes longer than local shops.

| Zone | Countries | Rate | Condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Malta | Malta | Standard (10–20 business days): €3.99 | under €40 |
| Malta | Malta | Free standard | €40 and over |
| EU | All EU countries | Standard (10–20 business days): €4.99 | under €50 |
| EU | All EU countries | Free standard | €50 and over |

Adjust once real supplier shipping costs are known (DSers shows them per product).

## Taxes (Settings → Taxes and duties)
- All prices include tax: **on**
- Malta VAT: 18% (standard rate)
- Selling to other EU countries over €10,000/year needs the EU OSS scheme. Check with your accountant.
- Charge VAT on shipping: on

## Checkout (Settings → Checkout)
- Customer contact: email or phone
- Customer accounts: optional
- Full name: require first and last name
- Company name: optional
- Address line 2: optional
- Shipping address phone: optional (couriers may need it)
- Email marketing: show checkbox, **not** pre-checked (GDPR)
- Order status page: add tracking link note ("Tracking appears within 3–5 days of ordering")

## Customer privacy
- Cookie banner: on, region EU/UK
