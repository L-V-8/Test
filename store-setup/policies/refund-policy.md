# Refund policy

*Draft – review before publishing.*

## Right to cancel (EU customers)
You can cancel your order for any reason within **14 days** of receiving it. To cancel,
email us at [[EMAIL]] with your order number. You don't need to give a reason.

## Returns
Send the item back within 14 days of telling us you want to cancel. Unless the item is
faulty or wrong, you pay the return postage. We will tell you the return address when
you contact us. Items must be unused and in their original packaging where possible.

## Refunds
We refund the full amount, including the original standard shipping cost, within 14 days
of receiving the item back (or proof you've sent it). Refunds go back to your original
payment method.

## Faulty, damaged or wrong items
Email us within 30 days with photos. We'll send a replacement or a full refund, and you
won't pay any return costs. Your legal guarantee rights (2 years in the EU) are not affected.

## Exceptions
Personalised items and items unsealed after delivery for hygiene reasons (e.g. earrings,
cosmetics) cannot be returned unless faulty.
