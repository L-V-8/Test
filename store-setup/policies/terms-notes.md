# Privacy policy and Terms of service

Use Shopify's built-in generators (Settings → Policies → "Create from template") for both,
then check these are filled in:

- Business name, Maltese address, email, VAT number (once registered)
- Privacy: GDPR rights (access, delete, correct), data shared with DSers/AliExpress and
  couriers for fulfilment, cookie use, the Maltese supervisory authority (IDPC, idpc.org.mt)
- Terms: governing law Malta, the 14-day right to cancel, link to refund and shipping policies
- Contact information page (Settings → Policies → Contact information) – required in the EU
