# Shipping policy

*Draft – review before publishing.*

## Processing time
Orders are processed within 1–3 business days.

## Delivery time
| Destination | Delivery time | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Malta | 10–20 business days | €3.99, free over €40 |
| Rest of the EU | 10–20 business days | €4.99, free over €50 |

Our products are shipped from our partner warehouses, some outside the EU, so delivery
takes longer than from a local shop. Items in one order may arrive in separate packages.

## Tracking
You'll get a tracking number by email within 3–5 business days of ordering.

## Customs
Orders to EU addresses include VAT at checkout. You will not pay extra import VAT.

## Lost or late orders
If your order hasn't arrived within 30 business days, email [[EMAIL]] and we'll send a
replacement or a full refund.
