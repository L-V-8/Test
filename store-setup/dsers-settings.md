# DSers settings

Plan: **Free** to start (bulk ordering + tracking sync included). Upgrade to Advanced
($19.90/month) only if you need more products or multiple suppliers per product.

## Settings → Pricing rule
- Pricing rule: on, apply to existing products too
- Basic rule:
  - Price = cost × 2.5 (items under €10 cost)
  - Price = cost × 2 (items €10–€30)
  - Price = cost × 1.7 (items over €30)
  - Compare-at price = price × 1.3 (optional; only if you're comfortable showing a "sale" — EU rules require the old price to have really been charged)
- Cents: end all prices in .99
- Include shipping cost in the cost: on

## Settings → Shipping
- Default shipping method: AliExpress Standard Shipping
- Prefer EU warehouse / "Ships from" EU country when available
- Country: Malta (add EU countries as default destinations too)

## Settings → Tracking
- Sync tracking numbers to Shopify: on
- Send Shopify shipping confirmation email to customer: on
- Mark orders as fulfilled when tracking is received: on
- Carrier: "Other", tracking URL: https://t.17track.net/en#nums=

## Settings → Order details
- Default phone number for AliExpress orders: on, use customer's
- Note to supplier: "Dropshipping order. No invoices or prices in the package, please. Thank you!"

## Settings → Synchronization
- Sync product stock from AliExpress: on
- When product is out of stock: set quantity to 0
- When product price changes: notify me (don't auto-change)

## Supplier checklist (per product)
- Store rating ≥ 95% positive, product rating ≥ 4.6
- 1,000+ orders
- Real customer photos in reviews
- Shipping to Malta under 20 days, tracked
